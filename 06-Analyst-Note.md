# Industrial Components Sector — Company Note
## Northwind Traders (NWT) | Rating: Overweight | Price Target: $148
**Meridian Research | February 24, 2026**

---

**Summary.** Northwind delivered FY25 revenue of approximately $4.0 billion (company-reported $4.2B; we apply a small adjustment for divested aftermarket SKUs), and we see three secular tailwinds and one execution risk heading into FY26.

**1. Nearshoring tailwind is real and underappreciated.** The Monterrey ramp is, by our channel checks, the smoothest large industrial nearshore project in our coverage. NWT's stated FY27 goal of reducing China exposure to <20% (from 34%) is credible. The European site decision (Poland vs. Czechia) is expected by mid-FY26 and could be a near-term catalyst.

**2. Demand forecasting investment is the right call.** Management's admission that ~$90M of margin was lost in FY25 to mix and inventory misalignment is unusually candid. The $35M FY26 commitment is significant for a company of NWT's size and signals seriousness. Our channel work suggests AWS is the incumbent on the data warehouse side (Redshift, since 2019) while Microsoft Azure has gained share in application and AI workloads since 2023. The forecasting platform decision is, in our view, **genuinely contested** between the two — not a foregone conclusion.

**3. OT security is now a board-level priority.** The November 2025 ransomware event affected two domestic plants for ~72 hours. Recovery was fast but the strategic message from the board is unambiguous: meaningful uplift in OT security spend in FY26 (~$25M committed). Splunk is the incumbent SIEM but, per our checks, **leadership is dissatisfied with detection coverage on the OT side**. The license renewal is in Q3 2026. Vendor decision likely to be made in Q2.

**Execution risk.** The CIO transition is a real risk. Marcus Wei's January departure was sudden and the interim CIO (Priya Raman) has not yet signaled whether the previously-scoped data modernization program survives in its current form. We expect clarity on the Q1 FY26 call.

**Implication for technology vendors.** FY26 is the most contested year for technology vendors in Northwind's recent history. We see three live decisions: (a) demand forecasting platform; (b) OT security platform; (c) M365 Copilot expansion from pilot to enterprise. Each is a multi-million-dollar decision and each appears to be open.
