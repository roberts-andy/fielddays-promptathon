# Northwind Traders, Inc. (NYSE: NWT)
## Q4 FY2025 Earnings Call Transcript — Excerpts
**Date:** February 6, 2026
**Participants:** Janet Okafor (CFO), Robert Lentz (CEO), Diego Marchetti (COO)

---

**Robert Lentz, CEO — Opening Remarks:**

"Good morning. Fiscal year 2025 closed with **total revenue of $4.2 billion**, up 6% year-over-year, with operating margins expanding 80 basis points. We had a difficult fourth quarter operationally — I'll let Diego speak to the November cyber incident in a moment — but the underlying demand environment for our industrial components business remained healthy.

Three things I want to highlight as we head into FY26.

First, **supply chain repositioning**. Phase one of our nearshoring strategy — the Monterrey, Mexico facility — is now at full ramp and is meeting cost and quality targets. In FY26 we will break ground on a second nearshore facility, this one in Central or Eastern Europe to serve our European OEM customers. We're finalizing site selection between Poland and Czechia this quarter. Our goal remains to reduce China-sourced revenue exposure from 34% to under 20% by the end of FY27.

Second, **demand forecasting**. We are not satisfied with our forecast accuracy. In FY25 we left, by our own estimate, $90 million of margin on the table due to mix and inventory misalignment. We are committing meaningful capital in FY26 to a modernized, AI-driven demand forecasting platform. Janet will speak more to the budget envelope.

Third, **cybersecurity**. As most of you know, on November 14 we experienced a ransomware event that affected production scheduling at two domestic plants for approximately 72 hours. We have fully recovered. We have engaged outside counsel and an independent IR firm. Our board has approved a substantial uplift to security investment in FY26, with particular focus on operational technology — our plant floor systems — where the November attack landed."

---

**Janet Okafor, CFO — Financial Detail:**

"Thank you, Robert. A few additions on capital allocation. Our FY26 capex plan of $310M includes approximately $40M for the European nearshore site, $35M dedicated to the demand forecasting and broader data platform build-out, and $25M earmarked for security modernization, with the OT security stream being the largest component of that.

One organizational note: as previously disclosed, our Chief Information Officer Marcus Wei departed in January to pursue an opportunity outside the company. We thank Marcus for his contributions. Priya Raman, formerly our Vice President of Data Platforms, is serving as interim CIO while the board completes a search. Priya is leading the FY26 technology agenda in the interim, including the demand forecasting initiative."

---

**Analyst Q&A — relevant excerpt:**

**Analyst (Morgan Stanley):** "Janet, can you talk about the technology stack decisions behind demand forecasting? Build versus buy, and is there a hyperscaler partner attached to that?"

**Janet Okafor:** "I'll let Priya weigh in next call. What I'll say from the finance seat is that we are explicitly looking for a partner who can give us forecasting outcomes — not a science project. We have an existing relationship with AWS on the data warehouse side and a growing relationship with Microsoft Azure on the application and AI side. Both are at the table. Decision criteria are accuracy, time-to-value, and total cost over a five-year horizon."

**Analyst (Bank of America):** "Diego, on the OT security side — what's the architecture you're aiming at?"

**Diego Marchetti:** "We're moving from a perimeter-and-pray posture to a true zero-trust model on the plant floor. That means continuous monitoring of every PLC, every HMI, every engineering workstation. We're evaluating two or three options. I'd rather not name vendors on the call."
